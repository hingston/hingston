Family

From these entries we can deduce:-

GEORGE HINGSTON married MARY TOWSON of Bigbury at Loddiswell on 14 Feb1741 (OS). They appear to have moved to Bigbury.

They had a son, GEORGE HINGSTON who was baptised 2 Apr 1743 at Bigbury.That George married GRACE WHITHEARE at Bigbury on 3 Jun 1774. Shewas born about 1748, the daughter of Rev. Elias Whiteheare (Curate at Bigbury).

George and Grace had a daughter ELIZABETH HINGSTON who was baptised27 Dec 1775 at Bigbury. Some crisis seems to have overcome the family since George Hingston (Yeoman) 33 yrs & Grace his wife, daughter ofRev Elias Whithear, 28 yrs, were both buried in one grave 19 Oct 1776. Did their daughter die as well? If not, who brought up the child? There is a quandary here that needs to be investigated.
