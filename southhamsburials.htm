Hingston South Hams burials from the Our4bears site.

This is a copy of the index of the Burials transcripts on the our4bears site (taken on 6th April 2012) including all the Hingston (and variant) records. Links to the relevant Hingston pages have been added.

I am grateful to Kathryn Barnett, Kara Oosterman and Mike Mitchelmore for permission to copy their data.

Included are: Blackawton 1697-1747; Charleton 1780-1850; Dodbrooke 1735-1826; East Allington 1779-1812; Halwell 1758-1828; Kingsbridge 1631-1812; Sherford 1713-1822; Slapton 1789-1800; Thurlestone 1687-1835; West Alvington 1720-1831; Woodleigh 1685-1812

Check the parish burial register for further details.
