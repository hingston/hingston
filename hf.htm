Hingston Tree HF

Descendants of Joseph Hingston, born about 1682.

This tree has largely been researched by Alf Hingston. I will pass on to him any comments.

Generation No. 1

1. JOSEPH HINGSTON was born Abt 1682, and died Aug 1743 in Thurlestone. He married ELIZABETH WORDEN 23 Apr 1710 in Thurlestone.

According to Alf Hingston there are two possible candidates to be this Joseph Hingston;

A. Joseph baptised Newton Ferrers 7/9/1682, son of Christopher baptised Newton Ferrers 13/10/1633, son of Bartholomew baptised Newton Ferrers 26/8/1610, son of George born about 1580 (tree HF-A).

B. Joseph baptised Modbury 28/2/1681/2, son of Joseph baptised Modbury 12/12/1647, son of Joseph born about 1620 (tree HF-B)

Children of Joseph Hingston and Elizabeth Worden are:

  • 2. WILLIAM HINGSTON , b. Jan 1711/12, Thurlestone; d. Feb 1803, Thurlestone.
  • 3. ABRAHAM HINGSTON , b. Jan 1712/13, Thurlestone; d. Mar 1795.
  • MARY HINGSTON, b. Feb 1714/15, Thurlestone.
  • 4. JOSEPH HINGSTON , b. Feb 1721/22, Thurlestone; d. Thurlestone.
  • JOHN HINGSTON, b. Mar 1722/23, Thurlestone; d. Mar 1722/23, Thurlestone.

Generation No. 3

5. GEORGE HINGSTON was born May 1737 in Thurlestone, the son of 2. William Hingston and Elizabeth (Jarvis) , and died Sep 1825 in Thurlestone. He married MARY COLLINS Mar 1760.

Two of George and Mary's children are candidates to be the William and John Hingston, believed to be brothers, who were Masters of ships transporting convicts to Australia.

Children of George Hingston and Mary Collins are:

  • MARY HINGSTON, b. Mar 1762, Thurlestone.
  • WILLIAM HINGSTON, b. Jul 1764, Thurlestone. He is a candidate to be HX#2, William , infamous master of the Hillsborough , in which many of the convicts died en route to Australia in 1798/99. This link is not proven.
  • JOHN HINGSTON, b. Apr 1767, Thurlestone. He is a candidate to be HX#1, John , master of the Speke , which transported female convicts to Sydney in 1808, but he was also involved in whaling and was one of the first white settlers to set up home in New Zealand. This link is not proven.
  • ELIZABETH HINGSTON, b. Sep 1769, Thurlestone.
  • GRACE COLLINS HINGSTON, b. Apr 1772, Thurlestone.
  • WALTER HINGSTON, b. May 1774, Thurlestone.

  • MARGARET HINGSTON, b. 1818; m. PHILIP HENRY NICHOLLS, 23 Jun 1839, Stoke Damerel. (The Nicholls line is being researched by Patricia Hard in Australia who supplied this information) Philip H Nicholls was born on 24 Feb 1820 in Sidmouth, Dev, to William Nicholls, a carpenter, and Rebecca Fawckner. Philip was a mariner and travelled the world and at one time was given an island to rule over in the Sulu Sea. He was offered the daughter of the King for his wife but Philip was already married and so left the islands while he still had a head on his shoulders. Philip and Margaret had several children, including Sarah born in 1856 in Hulme, Lancs. Philip later became a health inspector; in 1881 they were living at 19 Palin Street, Hulme, Lancs while by 1891 Margaret had died and he was living at Rusholme, Lancs. Philip died at 10 Stovell Ave, Crowcraft Park, Gorton, Lan on 24 Mar 1913 at the age of 93 years. Family lore says that Philip H married 3 times but as yet only one marriage has been found. They are believed to have had several children but details are not known.

Generation No. 5

12. ABRAHAM HINGSTON was born 29 Jun 1815 in Newton Ferrers, the son of 9. John Hingston and Sarah (Smith) , and died 26 Jan 1880 in Devonport. He married ELIZABETH MARTIN 26 Jul 1852 in Stoke Damerel. In the 1871 Census they were living at 1 Keppel Cottages in Devonport (which is part of Stoke Damerel Parish). At the same address there was also a pawnbroker’s shop. In 1881 and 1891 Elisabeth was living at the same address as a widow. She was described in a city directory as a Milliner.

Children of Abraham Hingston and Elizabeth Martin are:

  • 14. FRANK THOMAS MARTIN HINGSTON , b. 17 Mar 1862, Devonport; d. 9 Dec 1918.
  • ALFRED JOHN HINGSTON, b. 14 Sep 1863, Devonport; d. 8 Mar 1927; m. AMY JANE HANCOCK, 5 Feb 1891. In 1891 he was described as aged 27, a measuring recorder at the dockyard, by which time he was living at 74 Albert Rd, Stoke Damerel. At the same house were living Henry A. 49 & Jane 48 Hancock & Isabel 16 (parents & sister), all b. Devonport.
  • ELLEN ELIZABETH HINGSTON, b. 6 Feb 1865, Devonport; m. WILLIAM HENRY BENNETT, 26 Jul 1890. He was born c. 1861 in Devonport and was a boiler maker at the dockyard. They had three children, William, Frank and Ellen. In 1891 she was living in the Malborough area, between Salcombe and Kingsbridge. See Odds and Ends #45 .
  • ERNEST WALLACE HINGSTON, b. 1 Aug 1866, Devonport; d. 22 Jul 1870, Devonport.
  • EDITH MARY HINGSTON, b. 31 Jan 1868, Devonport; m. JOHN GEORGE RODGERS, 29 Aug 1886(where?) who was born 1866 at Woolwich in London. He was a ship's joiner so was presumably working at the dockyard. By 1901 the family had moved back to London where they were living at 31 Meridith St, Plaistow. Edith and John had 5 children, (Harry H, born 1888 in Devonport died Mar 1961 in West Ham; Nora Edith, born 1889 in Devonport; Flavia Rodgers, born 1897; Jack born 1902 in Plaistow; Frank Rodgers, born 1902 in Plaistow). Edith died Dec 1931 in West Ham, London and John died Jun 1952 in Plymouth.
  • MABEL FLORENCE ANNIE HINGSTON, b. 25 Sep 1869, Devonport; d. 29 Nov 1869, Devonport.
  • (INFANT - UNNAMED) HINGSTON, b. Feb 1871, Devonport; d. 2 Feb 1871, Devonport.
  • ERNEST HENRY WALLACE HINGSTON, b. 20 Oct 1872, Devonport; d. 1874.
  • ETHEL BLANCHE MARTIN HINGSTON, b. 29 Apr 1874, Devonport; m. SIDNEY ERNEST LAMB, 17 Aug 1899. In 1891 described as a Milliner’s Apprentice, single, living at Keppel Cottages.

16. WILLIAM HINGSTON , b. Oct 1841, Waterhead, East Portlemouth, the son of 10. William Hingston and Elizabeth Pritor. He seems to have married ELIZABETH WILLIAMS, since the eldest children are listed in the GRO with Williams as the mother's maiden name, but no suitable marriage has been found. He was a bricklayer and they seem to have lived in various places, presumably moving for work, and on 8 Jun 1870 they were living at 6 Golborne Gardens, Upper Westbourne Park in London. In May 1870 they had emigrated in the Medway to Quebec and settled at Parkdale near Toronto in Canada where two more (at least) children were born. They may have moved again becuase in the 1930 census in the US at Pleasantville, Atlantic Co., New Jersey, Eva is living with Arthur and his family and the census shows they became US citizens in 1882. Elizabeth died 1 Mar 1921 in Pleasantville, NJ.

Children:-

  • STELLA ELIZABETH HINGSTON born Kensington 4 Sep 1861 but baptised 8 Jun 1870 at St Luke's, Chelsea with her eldest siblings.
  • EVANGELINE (EVA) HINGSTON born Plymouth 5 May 1864. 2 trees on Ancestry with father William, born 1841 , Plymouth and mother Elizabeth Williams. Baptised with her sister on 8 Jun 1870 at St Luke's, Chelsea. In 1930 she was unmarried, a dressmaker, living with her brother Arthur in New Jersey where she later died.
  • WILLIAM HINGSTON born Newton Abbot 31 Dec 1865 and baptised with his sisters on 8 Jun 1870.
  • ARTHUR HINGSTON born in Canada c. 1876. He was a bricklayer and married, in about 1901, CARRIE who had been born c. 1878 in Pennsylvania. They had at least one daughter, DOROTHY M HINGSTON, born c. 1909, who married Joseph Badger, of New Jersey, born 1904, who ran an Auto Repair business. Arthur died Sep 1933
  • LOUISE HINGSTON born in Canada 3 Jun 1880 (some lists say Winifred Leonore Hingston)
