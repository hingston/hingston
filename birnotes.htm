Hingston families identified from FreeBMD and GRO 1837-1900 that are not yet associated with Trees

These notes refer to family groups that have been identified from the FreeBMD and GRO indexes which we cannot yet link to Trees. I will delete the notes when the information is incorporated into the trees.

1.  Hingston - Kingdon family.  Descendants of ROBERT HINGSTON born Plymouth about 1802.

ROBERT HINGSTON was born in Plymouth about 1802. He joined the Royal Navy on 12 Mar 1819, aged 16; father's name not shown. He married ANN WILLIAMS on 10 Mar 1830 at Stoke Damerel (DFHS index). Ann had been born in about 1803 at Bere Alston. Robert was not with Ann and their two children in 1841 - presumably he was away at sea. In the 1861 census they were living at Navy Row, Plymouth. He was 59, Boatswain, RN, born Plymouth; Ann 58 born Bere Alston; Sarah, daughter, 23, Devonport; Sarah Williams, mother in law, 88, Bere Alston.

Robert and Ann had two children:-

  • FREDERICK HINGSTON (see below)
  • SARAH ANN HINGSTON born Stoke Damerel Sep 1837 (GRO index)

1841: Ann Hingston 30 Frederick, 8 Sarah, 3 (Is this some sort of institution? where is it?). Presumably Robert is away at sea.

FREDERICK HINGSTON born 17 Dec 1831, bapt. 11 Jan 1832 at Beer Ferris, married MARY HONORIA KINGDON at Stoke Damerel Jun 1856. In 1851 he was a pupil at Wolborough (Newton Abbot). In 1861 at Bere Alston, Frederick, 29, Mary H, 28, Frederick W, 4, Robert W, 1

In the 1861 census in Stoke Damerel: Frederick, 29, born Bere Alston; Mary H, 28, Devonport; Frederick, 4 Robert,1.

By 1871 they were living at Portsea Island (Portsmouth)

Frederick and Mary had the following children:-

  • FREDERICK WESLEY HINGSTON born Stoke Damerel Mar Qtr 1857 (see below)
  • MARIA HONORIA PENGELLY HINGSTON born Stoke Damerel  Sep Qtr 1858 (probably died before 1861)
  • ROBERT WILLIAM HINGSTON born Stoke Damerel Jun Qtr 1860
  • ALFRED GEORGE HINGSTON born Stoke Damerel Mar Qtr 1862
  • CHARLES EDWIN HINGSTON born Stoke Damerel Mar Qtr 1864
  • ELIZABETH ANN HINGSTON born Stoke Damerel Mar Qtr 1866
  • KATE HINGSTON born c. 1870

FREDERICK WESLEY HINGSTON born Stoke Damerel Mar Qtr 1857. In Jun 1878 he married CAROLINE HANSFORD but it seems likely that she died, aged 37, in Portsea in Mar 1887. He then married ALICE FLORENCE CROUCH at Portsea in Sep 1888. In the 1901 Census. 15, Cressy Road, Town, Portsmouth: Frederick Hingston 46, Tailor born Devonport; Florence 36 born Portsmouth; Frederick, son, 20, Tailors Cutter born Portsmouth.

Frederick and Caroline seem to have had one son:-

  • FREDERICK ADOLPHUS HINGSTON born Sep 1880 at Portsea

4.  Hingstons around Salford. Descendants of William born Ireland 1804 and Bridget born Ireland ~1817

M0. WILLIAM HINGSTON was born in Ireland about 1804 and married BRIDGET, probably also in Ireland. They came to Manchester before 1859 since they were recorded in the 1861 Census in St Barnabas Parish in the city: William Hingston aged 57, Cotton Weaver born Ireland; Bridget age 44 born Ireland; Pheba age 16, Cotton Reeler born Ireland; William age 14, Cotton Weaver born Ireland, Alice age 2, born Manchester.

So their children include:-

  • PHEBA HINGSTON, born Ireland in about 1844
  • 1. WILLIAM HINGSTON , born Ireland in about 1846
  • ALICE HINGSTON, born Manchester in about 1859

M1. WILLIAM HINGSTON was born in Ireland sometime around in about 1846, the son of 0. William Hingston and Bridget. He presumably moved to the Manchester area with his parents before 1859. He married MARY CLAY there on the 1st Jun 1868 at the Cathedral and Parish Church in Manchester. He was 22, a Fireman and made his mark; his father was also William and a Cotton Twister. Mary was 19 and also made her mark; her father was Joseph Clay, a Labourer. Both William and Mary were living at 20 Emily Street, Ardwick. William and Mary later lived in Pendleton, which was an industrial area just north of Salford and Manchester. In the 1881 census at 245 Whit Lane, Pendleton, William was aged 36 (so born ~1845) a Cotton Mill Engineer Driver. His wife Mary was 37 born Manchester and their children Joseph 12 (born Manchester), Willie 10, Fanny 7, James 8, John 0 all born Pendleton. Two years later, now at 47 Whit Lane, William (now 59) is described as a Nightwatchman at Mill, with Mary 59, John 21 a Hairdresser, Phoebe 18 a Cotton Weaver, Simon 13, Abraham 9. All children born in Pendleton.

There are more entries to be found at Lancs OPC. Serach for Hingston. Entries added here up to 1910.

So the family of William and Mary probably is:-

  • 2. JOSEPH HICKSON born ~1869
  • 3. WILLIAM HICKSON born ~1871
  • FRANCES HINGSTON born Salford Jun 1874. She married, on 26 Nov 1898 at St George, Pendleton, Samuel Taylor age 28 a Labourer from Lower Broughton (part of Salford). Frances was living at 6 Eimeo St and her father was described as an Engineer and Joseph Hingston was a witness (Lancs OPC project). Eimeo St no longer exists but is shown on old OS 25 in maps as being at 53.4996N, 2.2887W, on the northern edge of Pendleton, near the area show as Greenstone Road on modern maps.
  • JAMES HINGSTON was born in Salford Jun Qtr 1876. He married JANE THOMPSON at Salford Jun 1897. In the 1901 census he was living at 5 James St, Pendleton, age 25 a Cotton Dyer; Jane 24 (both born Pendleton) but with no children. They had a daughter HANNAH ELIZA HINGSTON born Dec Qtr 1897 at Salford but she died within 3 months.
  • LEVI HINGSTON born ~1879. Birth not in FreeBMD but he died Jun Qtr 1880 aged 1 and was probably part of this family.
  • JOHN HINGSTON born Salford, bapt 11 Aug 1880 All Souls, Ancoats, Manchester. Living at 245 Whit Lane, father Firer-up. He married 25 Dec 1901 at St George, Pendleton, AGNES NEWSOME. He was a Hair Dresser of 47 Whit Lane. She was 22, living at 59 Cook St, daughter of Thomas Newsome, Platelayer. They had a daughter LILY HINGSTON bapt 5 Nov 1902 at St George, Pendleton, living at 47 Cook St, father Hair Dresser.
  • PHOEBE HINGSTON born Salford Mar 1884, she married 25 Nov 1905 at St Barnabas, Pendleton, Sydney Stanley Booth, 24, a Pavior. Both were living at 47 Whit Lane. His father was John Henry Booth, a Clerk.
  • ALICE HINGSTON bapt 9 Nov 1885 at St George Pendleton. Living at 245 Whit Lane, father an Engineer. Died same Quarter
  • ABRAHAM HINGSTON born Salford Dec 1886 died same Quarter.
  • SIMON (or SAMUEL) HINGSTON (Lancs OPC record shows Samuel - FreeBMD shows Simon) bapt. 25 Apr 1888 at St George, Pendleton, living at 33 Alderson St, father Engineer. Married (as Simon) 7 Sep 1907 at St James Broughton, Lancs, SUSIE WATKINSON. He was 22, Railway Carriage Cleaner of 54 Harriettta St. Her father was Richard Watkinson, a Labourer. Susie was 20, a Spinner of 35 Harrietta St.
  • ABRAHAM HINGSTON bapt 9 Dec 1891 St George, Pendleton, living at 57 Borough St, father Engineer.

M2. JOSEPH HINGSTON was born in Salford Mar 1869, bapt 5 Jan 1870 at St Thomas, Pendleton, the son of 1. William Hingston and Mary (Clay) (Not in GRO under Hingston but there was a Hickson). He married on 6 Jun 1895 SUSAN ASHTON at St John, Radcliffe, Lancs, father Engine Driver. Joseph was a Dyer living at 65 Janes St, Stand Lane.

They had at least two children:-

  • PHOEBE ALICE HINGSTON bapt 15 Apr 1896 at St John, Radcliffe, living at 8 Bank St, father Dyer.
  • IVY HINGSTON bapt 30 May 1900 at St John, Radcliffe, father Dyer, but she died and was buried 25 Aug 1900 at St John Radcliffe in the private grave of Thomas Ashton.

M3. WILLIAM HINGSTON was born in Salford c. 1871, the son of 1. William Hingston and Mary (Clay) (Not in GRO under Hingston but there were several similar names). He married MARGARET FOULKES at St Luke, Weaste near Manchester on 1 Jan 1890. Both William and Margaret were described as 21, with fathers deceased, but we know that William's father was still alive in 1901. In fact William was probably only 19 at most so this may have been a device to get married without his father's permission because Margaret was pregnant. He was described as a Labourer. Margaret's father was Moses Foulkes, a deceased Labourer. In the 1901 Census he was living at 63 Borough St, Pendleton, aged 29 (so born ~1872), a Locomotive Engine driver born Pendleton, with Margaret 29 and children William 9, Mary J 7, Samuel 3, Frances 0. (all born Pendleton).

Their children include:-

  • JOSEPH HINGSTON bapt 18 Jun 1890 at St George, Pendleton, living at 4 Gill St. Father's occupation Stripper. Joseph died same Quarter.
  • WILLIAM HINGSTON (not in FreeBMD) born ~1892 (from Census)
  • MARY JANE HINGSTON bapt 11 Apr 1894 at St George, Pendleton, living at 7 Kent St, father Fireman.
  • FRANCES HINGSTON bapt 6 May 1896 at St George, Pendleton, living at 34 Orchard St, father Fireman, died Mar Qtr 1897
  • SAMUEL HINGSTON bapt 16 Mar 1898 at St George, Pendleton, living at 34 Orchard St, father Engineer. Married: 8 Aug 1925 St John the Evangelist, Pendlebury, Lancs, SOPHIA STEPHENSON, 27, Spinster, Bank Lane, Father: George Arthur Stephenson, (deceased), Core Maker. He was 27, Moulder, Bachelor, Bank Lane Father: William Hingston, Crane Driver
  • AGNES FRANCES HINGSTON bapt 18 Jul 1900 at St George, Pendleton, living at 63 Borough St, father Engineer.
  • ALFRED HINGSTON bapt 10 Dec 1802 at St George, Pendleton, living at 28 Borough St, father Engine Driver.
  • SUSAN HINGSTON bapt 14 Feb 1904 at St George, Pendleton, living at 28 Borough St, father Engineer.
  • JAMES HINGSTON bapt 7 Mar 1906 at St George, Pendleton, 28 Borough St

There is one entry in the 1901 census that is in the same part of Salford as the family above but not in the GRO. But this John was apparently born 1870 and there is plenty of evidence that 1. William had a son John born ~1880 who was having children around 1900 so they cannot be from the same family. I can't find his birth, his marriage or his daughter's birth in FreeBMD. But he could though be the son of another son of M0. William.

M4. JOHN HINGSTON seems to have been born in about 1870 in Salford. The only evidence for him so far is in the 1901 Census at 14 Eimeo St Pendleton, where John Hingston age 31 (1870) Foreman Dyer, born Salford; Sarah age 30 born Radcliffe Lancs; Pricilla age 5 born Radcliffe

11.  Hingston - Hogan family.  Descendants of Thomas Hingston born Fermoy, Co. Cork in about 1843

F1. THOMAS HINGSTON was born in Fermoy, Co. Cork, Ireland in about 1843. Fermoy is in the north of Co Cork and some way from the known Hingston homes. The only possible link is the Thomas, son of HN49 , but we only have very scant information about that family and its source is uncertain. Thomas Hingston married JOHANNA HOGAN at Greenwich Dec 1861. She had been born about 1842 in Deptford. In the 1891 Census they were living at Tanners Hill, Deptford. Thomas Hingston 48 (1843) Bricklayers Scaffolder born Fermoy Co Cork, Johannah 49 Deptford; John Thomas 21 Bricklayers Scaffolder; Michael 18 Earth Crucible Worker; Patrick 16 Carriers Van Boy; Mary 14 Home Domestic; Annie 12; James 7. All children born Deptford. In 1901 and 1911. Thomas is in the Greenwich Union Workhouse.

Children:-

  • Possibly earlier children (Note big gap between wedding and first child)
  • 2. JOHN THOMAS HINGSTON born Deptford Mar 1870
  • 3. MICHAEL HENRY (HARRY) HINGSTON born Deptford (or possibly Fermoy) Jun 1872, an Earth Crucible Worker in 1901
  • PATRICK HINGSTON born Deptford Dec 1874
  • MARY HINGSTON born Deptford 1876 (not in GRO)
  • CATHERINE HINGSTON born Deptford Mar 1879
  • HANNAH (ANNIE)HINGSTON born Deptford Jun 1881
  • JAMES HINGSTON born Deptford Mar 1884

F2. JOHN THOMAS HINGSTON (Kingston in GRO) was born Deptford Mar Qtr, 1870, the son of 1. Thomas Hingston and Johanna Hogan. John married MARY ANN JOYCE at Greenwich Dec Qtr 1891. 1901 living at 18 St Johns Road Deptford; in 1911 Census John Thomas 41 Plumbago Crucible Maker; Mary Ann 40 born Kensington; Albert James 11; Ellen Violet 7; John Thomas 5; Thomas Charles 1. One child deceased

Possible children:-

  • MAUD AMELIA HINGSTON born Mar 1896 at Greenwich
  • ALBERT JAMES HINGSTON born Sep 1899 at Greenwich
  • ELLEN VIOLET HINGSTON born ~1904
  • JOHN THOMAS HINGSTON born ~1906
  • THOMAS CHARLES HINGSTON born ~1910

F3. MICHAEL HENRY (HARRY) HINGSTON was born Deptford (or possibly Fermoy) Jun 1872, the son of 1. Thomas Hingston and Johanna Hogan. He was an Earth Crucible Worker in 1891 and married ANGELINA ELLEN FOLKARD at St Saviour, Southwark Dec 1895. ( It is not clear whether he used the name Michael or Harry, and whether he was born in Deptford or Fermoy. It is just possible there were two separate people. )

Children:-

  • HENRY GEORGE HINGSTON born Greenwich Jun 1896
  • WILLIAM HINGSTON born Greenwich Dec 1897
  • MICHAEL JAMES HINGSTON born Greenwich Dec 1899

38. Hingston-Camp family. Descendants of William Hingston born 1818/1820, possibly Harbertonford

A1. WILLIAM HINGSTON age 43 in 1861 so born about 1818 at Aveton Ford(sic) [ Not Aveton Gifford - Harbertonford? ]; He married MARY ANN CAMP at Totnes Jun Qtr 1844. William and Mary Ann don't seem to be in the 1851 census in Devon but Philip age 6 and John age 2 are visiting with 19 year old Elizabeth Camp (head of household) at Bow in Ashprington. William died Jun 1871 at East Stonehouse aged 51. 1871, Mary, widow, plus Louisa, William, Henry and Emma.

A3. JOHN HINGSTON born about 1849 near Totnes 1. William Hingston and Mary Ann (Camp). The closest match in 1851 census is John 2 born Ashprington living with 19 year old Elizabeth Camp at Ashprington. He married SALLY ANN HAM at Stoke Damerel Sep 1870 (note after first two births). In 1871 Census: John 22 Fisherman born Totnes; Sally A 23 Plymouth; Sally A 1; Mary J Toms 12 Sister in Law. 1891 Census: John 42 Mariner Totnes; Sally A 43 Plymouth; John P 18 Mariner Plymouth; George 8. 1901 Census. Sally and son George (Labourer at Starch Factory) at 60 Cotehele Av Plymouth.

John's wife Sally Ann was born in 1848, the daughter of William Ham and Sally Hopper. William died in 1852 and Sally (the mother) married James Toms in 1856, who must also have died, because she married George French in 1863. The eldest child named here, 5. Samuel George was born 5 years before Sally Ann Ham married John Hingston, and listed as Samuel George Hopper Ham, so it is possible that John is not the father. Nevertheless, Samuel George seems to have been given Hingston as a surname when his mother married. It may be significant that in 1871 Samuel was living with his maternal grandparents, not with the rest of his new family.

Possible children:-

  • 5. SAMUEL GEORGE (HOPPER HAM) HINGSTON born Dec Qtr 1865 in Plymouth
  • SALLY ANN HINGSTON born Dec 1869 at Plymouth
  • 4. JOHN PHILIP WILLIAM HINGSTON born Mar 1873 at Plymouth of whom more later
  • LOUISA JANE HINGSTON born Dec 1874 at Plymouth
  • GRACE ROGERS HINGSTON born Mar 1880 at Plymouth
  • GEORGE ROGERS FRENCH born Dec 1882 at Plymouth

A5. SAMUEL GEORGE HINGSTON was born Dec 1865 at Plymouth when he was listed as Samuel George Hopper Ham, probably the son of Sally Ann Ham. He seems to have been given the surname Hingston when his mother married 3. John Hingston five years later, but it is possible that John was not his father. He seems, subsequently, to have lived with his maternal grandparents. In 1871 he was aged 5 (so born ~1866), born Plymouth, living with grandparents George and Sally French. In 1881 he was described as age 15 and a Mate (MS) [Merchant Service] living with George French 64, Master (MS). In 1891 he was at sea.

He married THIRZA HEARD at Plymouth Dec 1891. In 1901, they were with daughter, Edith M, 3 ( not Elizabeth ) and he is listed as a merchant seaman age 33 (so born c.1868).

Possible children:-

  • DAISY HINGSTON born Sep 1892 at Plymouth and died 1896.
  • EDITH MAY born Sep Q 1897 at Plymouth (in FreeBMD but not in GRO)

A4. JOHN PHILIP WILLIAM HINGSTON born Mar Qtr 1873 at Plymouth, the son of 3. John Hingston and Sally Ann (Ham). He married MARY PATTERSON at Plymouth Jun 1892. 1901 Census Mary Hingston wife 29 Plymouth; John P W 7; Ethel G 5; male baby 0. All born Plymouth. Was John away at sea?

57. Hingston-Paige family
