Hingstons at Diptford


This listing is an amalgam of information from WEH as listed in the Vine Tree and information from Gail Pullyblank, OPC for Diptford. WEH consistently uses Deptford but it is likely that he is conflating two places, Diptford in Devon, and Deptford in London. In Vine it is referred to as " near Brent ", and indeed Diptford is only 3 miles from South Brent (frequently known just as Brent). Many of the entries in Vine are indeed in the PRs at Brent, so the assumption must be that he did intend this to refer to Diptford. However, in his letter to the Tasmanian Hingstons , he refers to his great-grandfather, a lieutenant in the Royal Navy, being married in Deptford (that would have been in 1770). There is no suitable marriage in Diptford, or indeed Devon, and at this time WEH says his family were firmly based in Ireland; it is more likely that he was here referring to Deptford, next to Greenwich in London, which was the main London base of the Royal Navy.

WEH assumes that Jessie and his brother are incomers and founded the Hingston line in Diptford, but we know that the Devon Lay Subsidy Rolls of 1543/5 show that Thomas Hengston and Thomasina Hengston, a widow, were living here at that time.

Generation No. 1

JESSIE (JESSE) HINGSTON ( 757 in Vine ) According to Vine he was one of two brothers who settled in Deptford (sic - Diptford?), near Brent, about 1650, but he doesn't name the other brother. Vine says that he married MARGARET 2413. The PRs show that on 3 Feb 1653 Margaret wife of ??? Hingston was buried, and on 8 Mar 1654 Jesse Hingston was buried (Vine says he died 28 Mar 1654). If the dates for his daughter make sense, Jessie would have been born about 1600, or earlier.

Jessie and Margaret had two children

  • MARGARET HINGSTON ( Vine 758 ). Vine says she married Nicholas Grant 2407, 18 Dec 1654 (which implies she must have been born c. 1630).
  • HENRY HINGSTON ( Vine 759 ).
HUGH HINGSTON is not mentioned explicitly in Vine, but it is likely that he was the unnamed brother. The PRs show that his wife WILMONT was buried 2 Dec 1653 and Hugh was buried 26 May 1665. These names and dates match the entries for Hugh and Wilmont at the head of Tree HC .

Generation No. 2

HENRY HINGSTON ( Vine 759 ). Probably born c. 1630, son of Jessie and Margaret. Was married to JOAN (2505), and was buried 22 Jun 1676 (PR).

Henry and Joan had four children (probably born c. 1660-1676)

  • WILLIAM HINGSTON ( Vine 760 )
  • MARY HINGSTON ( Vine 761 ). She was buried 29 Sep 1662 (PR)
  • JOHN HINGSTON ( Vine 762 ).
  • MARY HINGSTON ( Vine 763 ) She was married on 4 May 1702 to ROBERT QUENLIN (according to Vine). PRs show his name as ROBERT QUARM, which is more plausible - Quarm is found in Devon. The PRs show she had a child ROBERT HINGSTON, bapt on 23 Apr 1702, a week before she married - it is likely that he was brought up as Quarm.

Generation No. 3

WILLIAM HINGSTON ( Vine 760 ) was the son of Henry 759 and Joan 2505, probably born about 1660. He married ANN 2506, and was buried 31 Jan 1693 (PR). It seems likely that Ann remarried; the PRs show that on 18 Aug 1695 ANN HINGSTON married JOHN PURDONNE and she seems to be the only candidate.

William and Ann had issue

  • WILLIAM HINGSTON ( Vine 764 ), bapt 20 Mar 1676 (PR)
  • ROBERT HINGSTON ( Vine 765 ) bapt 17 April 1681 (PR); died 14 August 1681 (not mentioned in PR).
  • JOHN HINGSTON ( Vine 766 ) bapt 23 Jan 1682 (PR).
  • ELIZABETH HINGSTON ( Vine 767 ) bapt 21 Jul 1685 (PR). Buried 29 May 1731 (PR). Vine says that she died without issue but there were two base born children in 1708 and 1725 to an Elizabeth and she is the only candidate amongst the Diptford Hingstons.
  • ROBERT HINGSTON ( Vine 768 ) born 25 Aug 1687 (Vine); PR says bapt 25 Dec 1687. Buried 28 Dec 1689 (PR)
  • JOHAN HINGSTON ( Vine 769 ) bapt 22 Sep 1689 (PR); buried 8 May 1696 (PR).
  • ANN HINGSTON ( Vine 770 ) bapt 22 Nov 1691 (PR).
JOHN HINGSTON ( Vine 762 ) was the son of Henry 759 and Joan 2505, probably born about 1765. He married to MARY 2369, of Deptford, who was buried 27 Aug 1701 (PR).

John and Mary had four children:

  • HONOR HINGSTON ( Vine 771 ), bapt 25 Jul 1697 (PR).
  • ANN HINGSTON ( Vine 772 ), bapt 18 Feb 1699 (PR). Buried on the 25th of the same month (PR)
  • JOHN HINGSTON ( Vine 773 ), born at Deptford, 10 March 1700 (not in PR).
  • ANN HINGSTON ( Vine 774 ), bapt 25 Mar 1701 (PR).
