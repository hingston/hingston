The following line makes sense of the above but there are many loose ends.

WILLIAM HINGSTON (born about 1781, buried 30 May 1846 at Loddiswell aged 66) married MARY LUCKHAM (born about 1776, died at Wisly (Wizaller?) and buried 17 Jun 1848 age 72) on 4 Sep 1801 at Loddiswell

Their children were :-

  • JENNY HINGSTON, bapt 25 Dec 1801
  • WILLIAM HINGSTON bapt. 25 Dec 1803 (see below)
  • JOHN HINGSTON 15 Dec 1805
  • REBECCA HINGSTON (twin) baptised 30 Jan 1808, buried 5 Mar 1809
  • SUSANNAH HINGSTON (twin) baptised 30 Jan 1808, buried 3 Feb 1808
WILLIAM HINGSTON (bapt 25 Dec 1803) married JOANNA PROWSE on 5 Aug 1829 (DFHS Index)

Their children were:-

  • WILLIAM HINGSTON baptised 20 Sep 1829, buried 07 Oct 1829 age 2 months
  • WILLIAM HINGSTON (bapt not found), born c. 1832 (from 1841 census, working as farm servant with James Yalland in 1851)
  • JOHN HINGSTON baptised 17 Aug 1834 in Loddiswell (father described as labourer) (in 1841 and 1851 censuses). Ag Lab.
  • HENRY HINGSTON (born 1838/39). (In 1841 and 1851 censuses)
  • MARY A HINGSTON (born 1840/41) (in 1841 and 1851 censuses)
  • ELIZABETH HINGSTON (born 1843)
  • CHARLOTTE HINGSTON (born 1848)
  • JANE HINGSTON (probably born 1850)l
