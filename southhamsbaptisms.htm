Hingston South Hams baptisms from the Our4bears site.

This is a copy of the index of the Baptismal transcripts on the our4bears site (taken on 6th April 2012) including all the Hingston (and variant) records. Links to the Our4bears parish pages are retained and links to the relevant Hingston pages have been added.

I am grateful to Kathryn Barnett, Kara Oosterman and Mike Mitchelmore for permission to copy their data.

The parishes included are: Bigbury 1727-1812 & 1839-1849; Blackawton 1748-1842; Charleton 1730-1812 & 1840-1861; Churchstow 1734-1812; Dodbrooke 1725-1812 & 1840-1881; East Allington 1724-1812 & 1837-1852; East Portlemouth 1671-1803 & 1837-1862; Halwell 1742-1812; Kingsbridge St Edmund 1798-1812; Kingsbridge Wesleyan 1849-1902; Loddiswell 1740-1812; Moreleigh 1695-1812; North Huish 1708-1833; Salcombe 1802-1867; Sherford 1756-1812; South Milton 1736-1812; South Pool 1750-1812; Stokenham 1725-1812; Strete 1836-1914; Thurlestone 1688-1852; West Alvington 1741-1812; Woodleigh 1685-1812 & 1840-1894

Click on the Parish link to visit the Our4bears parish page, where further information may be available. Click on the link in the final column to go to the relevant Hingston page, if one is available.

Dates before 1752 have been converted to the modern calendar.
