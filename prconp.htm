Hingstons in Cornwall - Parish Records

Sorted by Place then date

This is in the same format as the Devon listings and I may merge them at some stage in the future. The entries have been compiled from the Cornwall OPC Database . I have their permission to put this listing here and acknowledge their copyright of the original transcripts.

The database is not complete and is being updated periodicly. I will look for new Hingston entries when I have time. I have included various forms of the HINGSTON name, including HINGESTON, HINKSON, HINGSON and KINGSTON. Some of the latter probably are Kingstons, but some are certainly Hingstons. I have added links to the Hingston Trees elsewhere on this site and I hope to extend these in due course. I have doubts about the Mevagissey listings in Tree HL, but there are still gaps in the PRs.

This list has been compiled from Parish Register entries (and similar records). I have not yet ascertained whether the dates before 1751 are Old Style (as used in most registers) or not. The entries have come from a number of sources so the format may vary. Where possible a link is given to the relevant family in one of the trees.

If you find any errors, or have an equivalent list for other parishes, please get in touch with me at cjb@eng.cam.ac.uk
