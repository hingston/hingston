Hingstons in North Huish

1662. There appears to have been a Hingston entry in the torn page at the top of 1662

Feb 1662. Thomas the sonne of Edward Hingston and Joan baptised

9 Nov 1664. Thomas (?) Hingston was buried (there is a tear across the name)

15 Nov 1665. John the sonne of Edward Hingston and Joan baptised

2 Feb 1668. Sibella (?) daughter of Edward Hingston and Johan baptised

19 Oct 1672. Edward Hingston was buried

Jul 1674 .... Leight and Mary Hingston were married (page torn)

26 Oct 1677. Thomas Hinkston and Jane Adams of Holbeton were married

12 Feb 1681. Johan Hinston, base born chold of Johan Hinston

10 Apr 1692 John Hingston and Jane Damerell were married

(baptisms 1692-1697 very faint and could well have missed some entries)

23 Dec 1693. Jane daughter of John Hingston and Jane baptised

7 May 1695. Mary daughter of John Hingston and Jane baptised

1 Jun 1695. daughter of John Hingston and Jane buried

6 Sep 1697. John son of John Hingston and Jane baptised

16 Oct 1702. Richard the son of John Hingson and Jane baptised

12 Jan 1705. Elizabeth daughter of John Hinckson and Jane baptised

31 Aug 1712. Joan Hingston buried

5 Nov 1734. William Treby and Mary Hinckson were married

28 Feb 1734 (OS). John Hinckson alias Gibbs and Bridget Oldreive were married with banns

16 Nov 1735. John son of John Gibbs, alias Hinckson and Bridget baptised

19 Aug 1739. Bridget daughter of John and Bridget Gibs was baptised

6 Nov 1743. Bridget Gibs (alias Hinckson) was buried

30 Dec 1744. John Hinckson alias Gibs and Elizabeth Hext were married with banns

30 Jan 1755. John the son of John and Agnes Inckson (sic) baptised

(no further Hingston or Gibbs entries to 1786)
