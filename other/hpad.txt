The Hingston family tree of Allen and Dymond (1851)

This text accompanies GEDCOM file HPAD.GED dated May 1998.

This tree is included in C H Fox's 'A Short Genealogical Account of 
Some of the Various Families of Fox in the West of England', printed 
in 1864 and now in the collection of the West Country Studies Library 
in Exeter. It has no information dated later than 1855. It attempts 
to show the descendants of Andrew Hingston (d.1643) of  Scotscombe 
(now Scobbiscombe), Holbeton, Devon and his wife Grace (d.1675) of 
Kingmore. Both male and female lines of descent are shown. The title 
and additional information from the chart are reproduced on the next 
page.

Although the introduction refers to those 'registered at Holbeton', 
indicating that the Holbeton parish registers had been consulted, 
and to the Quarterly Meeting Registers (of the Society of Friends), 
there are relatively few dates from the mid-eighteenth century onward 
and it is clear that it relied principally upon family sources.

The document was researched and copied by Dr Alison Simkins in May 1998, 
and transferred to GEDCOM format by Andrew Hingston. A few obvious 
printing errors were corrected, but it was otherwise reproduced as it 
was. You are free to use the information, but to help future researchers 
please treat it as a source document, and remember that it is supplementary to oth
er documentary sources including registers, census data, wills, etc not 
available to Allen and Dymond. If you do make any alterations directly 
to the attached GEDCOM file please do not pass it to others in its 
amended form under the same file name and without making clear your 
alterations.

The original chart is fragile and for conservation reasons the West 
Country Studies Librarian has decided not to permit further copying. 
If you wish to check possible errors in the transcription you are invited 
to send queries to Andrew Hingston  (ahingst@ibm.net or by post to 
19 Mount Parade, York, YO24 4AP, England) who has a photocopy.
