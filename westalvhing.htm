West Alvington Hingstons


It appears, potentially, that we have found the missing Hingstons that I have been seeking since the 1960s. I had not, hitherto, looked for them in West Alvington since there appeared to be none there in the 19th century. There are many more Hingston in the bit that is very difficult to read from the beginning of the registers through to the 1670s. Other entries have been supplied by Bob Shepherd. Entries that start with HINGSTON are from Ray Osborne's index for baptisms where they differ from my entries. Some of the entries here have been attached to TRee HA but it is clear that there are many other families that need disentangling.
