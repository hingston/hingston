Hingston Tree HC

Descendants of Hugh Hingston of Aveton Gifford.


This tree represents several generations of Hingston living at Aveton Gifford. The name Hugh seems to run through it. There is an assumed extension via a Customs Officer who later moved to Belfast, from whom there are several descendants. Pam Goodburn (PG) has sent me some information about the first three generations but this information was passed to her by her mother and it is not clear where it comes from. There are clearly entries in the parish registers at Aveton Gifford and Bigbury that almost certainly relate in some way to these entries, but which are not yet tied in.

The tree has been significantly updated and extended by including the results of WEH's study . As a result the families 9. to 14. have been added. 14/4/2013.

C J B

4. NATHANIEL HINGSTON ( 1597 in WEH ) was baptised 9 May 1807 at Thurlestone, the son of 3. Hugh Hingston and Mary (Avery) . He married JANE CROKER 29 Jan 1833 at St Petrox, Dartmouth; she was described as being of the parish of Stoke Fleming - he was described as a sojourner so did not come from the parish; she was buried 20 Jun 1882 (in Belfast?), age 74. Nathaniel was a preventive coastguard officer, which presumably involved moving around various postings on the south coast, before becoming a Customs House Officer at Belfast. Nathaniel joined the coastguard on 6 Sept 1835, serving on the ship ‘Hawke’ (source PRO ADM 6 199). In the 1841 census Nathaniel was a boatman for the coastguard, living on St Agnes island in the Isles of Scilly; his place of birth is given as ‘not in Cornwall’. He was described to WEH as "A very tall and fine looking man with flaxen hair, and with a strong family likeness."

Most of the subsequent information regarding this family come from Richard Hingston who can be contacted via his brother Rowland <rowland.hingston@AES.com>, but some is from Sarah Cotter <scc27@tutor.open.ac.uk>.

Richard married, secondly, in 1887 JANE PIGNEY at Epsom. She already had a child Ellen Jane Pigney, born 1876, who did not adopt the name Hingston (she married (as Pigney) in 1900 in Wandsworth). They do not appear to have had children of their own but in 1891 at York Road, Lambeth, Richard's children with Mary were living with them, as was Ellen Pigney age 15 and William Hingston, a boarder, age 24 and a Carpenter. He is almost certainly Richard's nephew, son of Nathaniel.

5. WILLIAM HINGSTON ( 1598 in WEH ) was baptised 25 Oct 1840, St Agnes, Isles of Scilly, the son of 4. Nathaniel Hingston and Jane (Croker) . He married AGNES MARGARET GILLES 3 Apr 1871, the daughter of James Gilles and Sarah (Kerr) of Liverpool, born 19 Feb 1843. She died 14 Jun 1925 in Belfast. William died 2 Nov 1922 and is buried in Belfast City Cemetry, Falls Road, Belfast, where there is a memorial to the whole family. William was a joiner - he was apprenticed to Robert Hickson and Company in 1854. The yard was acquired by E J Harland in 1858. William became the head of the Joiners, Cabinet-Makers and Woodworking machine men (some 2000 to 3000 men) in the Harland and Wolff Shipyard. He retired in 1908 after 54 years service.

The children of William Hingston and Agnes are:-

  • MINNIE ELIZABETH HINGSTON (1679 in WEH) born 9 Dec 1871 (twin), died 1 Dec 1950, Belfast. Minnie became the housekeeper to the family, including William, Agnes and Clara who did not marry and lived together at "Norton", 6 Hawthornden Road, Knock.
  • SARAH LOUISA HINGSTON (1680 in WEH) born 9 Dec 1871 (twin), died 17 Dec 1893, Belfast
  • WILLIAM GILLES HINGSTON (1681 in WEH) born 4 Aug 1873, died 7 Jan 1949, Belfast. He was an ironmonger and worked for Patterson's Hardware.
  • GEORGE HENRY HINGSTON (not shown in WEH) was born 1874 (he is only shown in the tree of Sarah Cotter, not that of Rowland Hingston). He is known to have married and had children but I have no details.
  • AGNES MARGARET HINGSTON (1682 in WEH) born 13 Aug 1875, died 11 Jul 1954, Belfast. Postmistress at Mountpottinger Post Office.
  • ELLA JANE HINGSTON (1683 in WEH) born 19 Oct 1877, married JOHN COTTER, Ballynafeigh Methodist Church, Belfast. Two sons, William and John. Died 17 Aug 1923, Belfast.
  • JOHN HERBERT HINGSTON (1684 in WEH) born 27 May 1880, Belfast. Married ETHEL HULSE 30 Nov 1908, Knock Methodist Church, Belfast; she was born 9 Oct 1883, died 18 Oct 1961, Belfast). They had two sons William and Joseph. He died 1 Jun 1941, Belfast. He was an Engine Fitter, later Marine Superintendent, Harland and Wolff until 1922. Later joined John Kelly Ltd, a coal importing business in Belfast. He was a keen swimmer and represented Ireland at Water Polo.
  • CLARA ANNIE HINGSTON (1685 in WEH) was born 7 Oct 1884, died 16 Jan 1936, Belfast.

There was also a JANE CROKER HINGSTON born Belfast 1871 (ref 11 239) who was presumably related here in some way.
